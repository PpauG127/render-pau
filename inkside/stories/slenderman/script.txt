Two girls. One knife. And a man who doesn't exist.
This is the story of how a picture made as a joke… almost killed a little girl.

It started on an internet forum, where people competed to make the scariest fake photos.
One user posted two old black-and-white pictures of kids playing in a park.
Nothing strange… until you look behind them.
Something tall. Too thin. A black suit. And where its face should be… nothing.
They called him the Slender Man.

It was supposed to be a joke. But the internet doesn't do jokes.
Soon, everyone was writing about him. Videos. Games. Stories that got darker and darker.
And the legend grew its own rules.
He lives deep in the woods. He watches children.
And if you want him to spare you and your family… you have to serve him.

Most people read those stories, got a little scared, and closed the laptop.
Two girls didn't.

Morgan and Anissa were twelve. And they were completely sure that Slender Man was real.
Real enough to be watching them. Real enough to hurt their families if they disobeyed.
So they made a plan. They worked on it for months.
And the plan had a name. Their friend… Payton.

It was Morgan's birthday. A sleepover. Roller skates, frozen yogurt, a normal girls' night.
Payton had no idea that, the whole time, her two friends were waiting.

The next morning, they took her to the woods.
"Let's play hide-and-seek," they said. "Lie down. Cover yourself with leaves. Nobody will find you."
And Payton did.
She lay there, under the sticks and the leaves… and Morgan pulled out the knife she had brought from her kitchen.
Nineteen times.

Then they walked away. Deeper into the woods. Looking for him.
They believed Slender Man had a mansion hidden in the forest… and that now, finally, he would accept them.

But Payton wasn't dead.
Bleeding, she stood up, grabbed the trees one by one, and dragged herself out of the woods… until a cyclist found her by the road.
One of the wounds had stopped less than a millimeter from a major artery of her heart.
Less than one millimeter.
And when she woke up after surgery, one of the first things she asked was:
"Did they get them?"

They did. That same day, police found the two girls walking along the highway… still on their way to find him.

Both were sent to a psychiatric hospital.
Years later, they were let out… and one of them ran away.
When police found her, sleeping outside a truck stop, she told them she had done something really bad.
And that they could just… Google her.

Payton survived. And today she wants to work in medicine… like the doctors who saved her life.

Slender Man was never real. Somebody made him up in an afternoon, as a joke.
But for two girls… he was real enough to kill for.
So who's really to blame? The internet? The parents? Or… him?
